RealView analyzes only visible page text, images, and video frames, on your device. Password fields, form inputs, and editors are skipped. Nothing is uploaded or stored beyond the current page view; images hosted on another domain are refetched from that same host, without credentials, so their pixels can be measured.

Detection reports signals and likelihood, never proof. Every treatment is reversible with the reveal control.
